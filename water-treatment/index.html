01 — Water Solutions

Redefining water solutions

Sustainable, customised water treatment for commercial, industrial and residential needs — turning any source, including brackish and seawater, into pure fresh drinking water.

Request a system design
The systems

Two ways to get clean water on site.

Portable Water Treatment Plant

Custom systems turning any water source — including brackish and seawater — into pure, fresh drinking water.

  • Fresh drinking water from seawater and brackish sources
  • Custom designed for your site
  • Scalable, long-term solution
  • Reliable and sustainable water supply

Portable Water Purification

Safe drinking water in emergencies, rural areas and remote communities, deployable in hours rather than months.

  • Fast and easy deployment
  • Portable, modular design
  • Safe drinking water anywhere
  • Low operating and maintenance cost
How it works

Comprehensive filtration and water treatment expertise.

Intake, treatment and delivery, engineered as one stack and supported for the life of the asset.

Custom water treatment plants

We design and manufacture treatment plants for needs that range from large-scale industrial to compact residential. Smart options such as remotely managed filtration keep them running efficiently while lowering their environmental impact.

Innovative filtration systems

Advanced filtration built on proven technology, modular and scalable, and straightforward to operate — suited equally to rapid deployment and to permanent installation.

Integrated services, end to end

Initial assessment, system design, fabrication, installation, testing and after-sales support. One accountable party across the whole sequence means smoother execution, less risk carried by you, and dependable performance over the asset's life.

Targeted applications

Industrial facilities, commercial developments, remote communities, coastal areas and emergency response. Each solution is matched to its application rather than adapted to it, so water quality and running efficiency both hold up.
