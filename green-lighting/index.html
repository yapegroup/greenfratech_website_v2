Outdoors — Street Lighting

Then the road outside it.

A full LED street lighting range built to Japanese standards through Iwasaki Electric's Malaysian arm — mains-fed or wholly off-grid, SIRIM certified, from a 30W access road to a 280W highway.

ELAP SL-EY636

The workhorse. Eleven outputs on one body, tool-less entry for maintenance, and the highest efficacy in the range.

  • 30W – 240W, eleven outputs
  • Up to 38,826 lm at 162 lm/W
  • IP66, IK09, CE & CB
  • Power factor 0.95 – 0.98
  • Main road, residential and smart-lighting ready

LEDioc SL-EY686

The specified option, where a project calls for SIRIM paperwork and heavy surge protection.

  • 35W – 280W across four bodies
  • Up to 38,347 lm at 137.4 lm/W
  • SIRIM PC004404, EMAL listed
  • 50kV / 25kA surge protection
  • L70 > 50,000 h at 50°C ambient
  • ±15° tilt, 1–10V / PWM / DALI dimming

SURIA SL-EY628

The widest choice of outputs in the range — fourteen steps, so the fitting is matched to the road rather than rounded up to it.

  • 30W – 180W in fourteen steps
  • 3000K or 6000K, others on request
  • Type II distribution
  • Eyelite, by Iwasaki Electric

SORAA all-in-one solar

Off-grid. Panel, LiFePO₄ battery and fitting as one unit, for roads where bringing a cable is the expensive part. Three models, 40W to 120W.

  • SSL-EY632 — 60W, 80W, 120W
  • SSL-EL631 — 80W, 13,600 lm
  • SSL-EY633 — 40W, up to 200 lm/W
  • Two days' autonomy, 12h a night
  • Monocrystalline panel, glass LED module
Sizing the solar

No trench, no meter, no cable run.

Each SORAA unit carries its own generation and storage, sized so it still lights the road after two overcast days. Which model you need is a question of road width and mounting height rather than of what the grid can reach.
