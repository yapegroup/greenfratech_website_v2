Repair and maintain

VIACORE®, the ultimate cold asphalt repair

100% environmentally friendly All load categories All-weather use

A high-performance, cold-applied asphalt for quick and durable road repairs. Mix with water, apply and compact — no heating required. It hardens like hot mix asphalt, which makes it right for potholes, cracks and surface levelling wherever bringing a hot plant to site is not worth it.

Consumption

Roughly 22–24 kg per cm of depth, per m².

Cleaning

Biodiesel or rapeseed oil.

Storage

Cool and dry, out of direct sunlight.

Applications

  • Road repairs, all load categories, 2.5 cm to 8 cm per layer
  • Pothole & core hole filling
  • Levelling height differences & drainage repairs
  • Low-traffic and high-traffic roads, including highways
  • Manhole and utility trench reinstatements
  • Industrial yards, factory access roads and parking areas

How to apply

  1. Clean & dry the surface
  2. Mix Viacore with 2 L of water
  3. Pour the mixture on the surface
  4. Spread the mixture using a rake
  5. Compact the surface using a tamper

Seal the joints

REFUG® 100, reliable joint sealing

100% solvent-free formula Traffic-ready in 1 hour All-weather use

A cold-applied sealing compound for cracks, joints and bonding work on both asphalt and concrete. Fast-curing and weather-resistant, it closes the seams a repair leaves behind — and holds road studs and roofing sheets just as well.

  • Cold-applied & easy to use
  • Fast curing
  • Weather-resistant
  • Long-lasting & flexible
  • Eco-friendly

Consumption

Roughly 1.2 kg per dm³ of joint.

Cleaning

Biodiesel or rapeseed oil.

Storage

Cool and dry, protected from frost.

Applications

  • Seals cracks, seams and joints on asphalt & concrete
  • Bonds roofing sheets and films
  • Adheres road studs securely

How to apply

  1. Attach the mixing nozzle to the cartridge
  2. Use a dispenser to fill the cleaned joint
  3. Smooth with a spatula & sprinkle with chippings or sand
