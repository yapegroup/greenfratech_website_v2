01 — Bamboo Geotextile

Build on ground that couldn't be built on.

A bamboo-and-geotextile composite that lets heavy construction sit directly on soft subsoil — proven in mangrove swamps, peat swamps and marine clay grounds.

  • Proven on deep and critically soft ground
  • Cost-effective against conventional consolidation
  • Renewable, bamboo-based composite

05 — Green Cleaning

And every surface it touches.

Tersano turns tap water and oxygen into a disinfectant stronger than bleach, made on demand. Our patented grease trap keeps fats and oils out of the sewer.

  • Safe and non-toxic for staff and occupants
  • No packaging, no chemical waste stream
  • Drops into existing cleaning routines
  • Patented grease trap — MySTI certified, JKR approved
