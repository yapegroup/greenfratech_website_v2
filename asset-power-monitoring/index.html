01 — In operation

Asset & Power Monitoring System

Smart building monitoring, from data to insight. One connected system for electrical power and critical assets, from the sensors on the board to the dashboard in your pocket.

Safer buildings Higher efficiency Lower operating costs Sustainable operations
System architecture

From data to insights — a connected, intelligent building.

Five stages, each one standard hardware or standard protocol. Nothing in the chain locks you to a single vendor.

  1. Field sensors & meters — power meters and CTs, environmental sensors, equipment sensors and other building systems, fitted across your electrical and building assets.
  2. IoT gateway / data logger — collects and converts protocols on site, processes locally, transmits securely and keeps storing when the link drops.
  3. Communication — Wi-Fi, 4G/5G or Ethernet, carried over MQTT or HTTPS.
  4. Cloud platform — secure server, real-time storage, analytics and scalable infrastructure, reachable from anywhere.
  5. Web & mobile dashboard — real-time monitoring, alerts and notifications, reports and analytics, multi-user access, on desktop and phone alike.

  • Floor

Detect & alert

  • Abnormal consumption
  • Overload and overcurrent
  • Voltage abnormality
  • Low or high power factor
  • Harmonics and power quality issues
  • Equipment trip or failure
  • Custom alert settings

What the system records per asset

Every asset carries the same record, so a lift and a fire pump are read the same way — and a fault on either one reaches you through the same alert.

  • Operating status (on / off)
  • Runtime and operating hours
  • Temperature and vibration, where applicable
  • Fault and trip status
  • Maintenance records
  • Alerts and notifications

02 — Pre-construction

Togal.AI — AI takeoff for pre-construction

Where the monitoring system watches a building that already exists, Togal.AI works on the one you are still pricing.

Measured from the drawing, not by hand

Togal.AI reads a digital plan set and detects, measures and compares the areas on it automatically. The estimator stops tracing and starts checking — the quantities arrive, and the judgement stays with the person.

98% Accuracy on floor plans, as published by Togal.AI
5× Faster project takeoff, on Togal's own figures

What it does

Togal Button

One press and the AI handles the clicking and counting that used to take an estimator hours or days.

AI-driven search

Draw a box around one symbol and it finds and counts every other instance of it, by image, text or pattern.

Instant labels

Upload a plan set and it names and organises the whole thing in seconds, instead of someone renaming sheets all morning.

Real-time collaboration

Plans, changes and markups live in one place, so a team in three offices is working on one set rather than four.

Togal Chat

A built-in takeoff assistant: ask it questions about the plans, verify quantities, catch errors and draft RFPs.

Data export

Quantities go straight out to your estimating software or to a spreadsheet, so the takeoff is not trapped in the tool.
