02 — Bamboo Geotextile

The Geobamtile composite system

Green, dependable soft soil treatment for reliable, cost-effective infrastructure on the most challenging ground conditions — proven in mangrove swamps, peat swamps and marine clay.

Discuss your ground
Why bamboo-geotextile

Build directly on top of soft subsoil.

Proven on critically soft ground

A green and dependable ground treatment method that allows heavy construction to be built directly atop soft subsoil.

Cost-effective by comparison

Cheaper and faster than conventional consolidation, with no long preload waiting period holding up the programme.

Renewable by construction

Bamboo is a fast-renewing local material, so the reinforcement itself carries a fraction of the embodied carbon.

Engineered, not improvised

Soft ground engineering expertise behind every layout: spacing, depth and composite selection are designed per site.

The method

Four steps from swamp to platform.

  • Excavation works on soft subsoil
  • Precision bamboo installation
  • Laying of the geo-textile composite
  • Reliable and sustainable ground support
