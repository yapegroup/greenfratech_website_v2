lotus PRO V9, by Tersano

Tap water in, the janitor's closet out.

The lotus PRO V9 is what makes the solution the rest of this page runs on. Cold mains water passes through an SAO cartridge that strips the minerals which would shorten ozone's life, then through the dispenser, which infuses it with ozone. What comes out of the nozzle is a cleaner, sanitiser and deodoriser in one, made on the spot at up to 11 litres a minute.

It replaces up to 90% of the chemicals in a cleaning cupboard, and when the job is done the ozone reverts to water and oxygen. There is nothing to rinse off and nothing to dispose of.

Not to be confused with the Lotus WTS further down this page. Same name, different job: that one keeps biofilm out of the water lines themselves.

Made on site, on demand

Nothing to order, store or mix. The system needs cold potable water, a drain and a standard 120–220V outlet.

Tested and approved

Up to 99.99% reduction of organisms including E. coli, Salmonella, Listeria and Influenza A. Approved by Health Canada, USDA, EPA, FDA, Green Seal and HACCP.

Reports on itself

The V9 sends usage to the iClean dashboard over Wi-Fi — cartridge life, cleaning times, and chemical and carbon reductions you can put in a report.

Lotus WTS, by Tersano

Treated water is only clean until the pipe undoes it.

Most of the bacteria in a water system are not floating in the water — they live in biofilm on the inside of the lines, where a conventional filter never reaches them. Lotus works on the line itself, running automated cleaning cycles with aqueous ozone so a film never gets the chance to establish.

The ozone reverts to oxygen and water when the cycle finishes, so nothing is left in the line to flush out, taste or dispose of.

What it does

  • Active Microbial Protection — blocks biofilm before it forms
  • Guards against Pseudomonas, coliform and biofilm regrowth
  • Automated forward-flush and back-flush cycles
  • Self-cleaning filter set, changed annually
  • RFID-tagged filters report their own life and performance
  • Protects taste and clarity in whatever it feeds

The cost of leaving it alone

A sediment filter has no defence against a pathogen, and a carbon filter stops being part of the solution the moment it saturates: from then on it is a nutrient-rich surface that a colony can feed on. Biofilm is what makes that stick — it resists chemical cleaning and survives freezing, which is why scrubbing a line clean does not keep it clean.

0 Chemical residue — ozone reverts to oxygen and water
Why it is different

A filter that traps is a filter that feeds.

Conventional systems catch contaminants and hold them in the line. Lotus filters, then cleans itself and everything downstream of it, on a cycle nobody has to remember to run.

It is worth reading the fine print on whatever is fitted now: most filtration systems quietly assume somebody will disinfect them by hand on a schedule. That assumption is where the maintenance hours go, and where the biofilm comes back. Tersano began as a water treatment company twenty-five years ago and the technology has been through both laboratory and field trials since.

Grease Trap System — Razak Three, Type B

Stop the grease before the sewer does.

Patented MySTI certified Approved by JKR

A high-performance grease trap that separates fats, oils and grease from kitchen wastewater before it reaches the sewerage system. It is a pre-treatment stage, fitted between the sink and the drain, and it is the cheapest point at which a blockage can be prevented.

The Razak Three (Type B) is built for premises with more than one food outlet — canteens, restaurants, residential kitchens and food courts — where grease and solid waste otherwise accumulate faster than a pipe run can clear them, taking odour and blockages with them.

Prevents clogging

Traps grease and solid waste before either of them enters the sewer system.

Reduces maintenance cost

Fewer blockages downstream, and less pipeline maintenance to budget for across the year.

Improves hygiene

Cuts the odour that standing grease produces and keeps the kitchen environment clean.

Easy cleaning

Designed so collected waste lifts out and the tank rinses down, with no tools and no strip-down.

Compliant solution

Meets JKR and JPWP requirements for pre-treatment systems ahead of a sewerage connection.

Durable and reliable

High-toughness resin and stainless steel, for a component that sits in hot, greasy water every service.

Resin sections are moulded at 5.0 mm and 3 mm. The 40 mm inlet and outlet are the standard size for a commercial kitchen waste run, so the trap drops into an existing layout without reworking the pipes.

How it works

  1. Wastewater inlet. Kitchen wastewater flows into the trap through the inlet pipe.
  2. Grease separation. Fats, oils and grease float to the top; solid waste settles at the bottom.
  3. Clean water outlet. The treated layer between the two leaves for the sewerage system.

Emptying it

  1. Open the lid of the grease trap.
  2. Remove and dispose of the collected grease and solid waste.
  3. Rinse the tank, and refill if necessary.

No tools, no dismantling and nothing to dose. How often it needs doing is set by covers served rather than by a fixed interval — a single canteen and a food court will not be on the same schedule.

Why pre-treatment is asked for

Grease does not stay liquid once it leaves a warm kitchen. It cools in the pipe run and hardens against the wall, catching solids as it goes, which is how a sewer connection narrows long before anyone reports a problem. A trap at the source is what the JKR and JPWP requirements exist to put there.

Independently evaluated

Measured over four months, not estimated.

Universiti Teknologi Malaysia monitored the grease trap system (brand: Rekabina Ran, model: Razak Two — the predecessor of the Razak Three above) from 22 February to 28 June 2021, analysing thirty samples of real kitchen wastewater taken at the inlet and the outlet.

What it adds up to

  • Solids removed at 98% on average, so less settles downstream
  • COD down 90% and BOD₅ down 89% — a far lighter organic load
  • Fewer blockages and less odour reaching the sewerage system
